MeetFair

Every distributed team has the same fight: someone is always on the 7am call or the 10pm call, and it's usually the same someone.

MeetFair makes the tradeoff explicit. Each person enters their timezone and the local hours they'll actually take a meeting. The app finds every UTC hour where everyone fits, then ranks them by total pain - how far outside normal work hours each slot pushes people - so the fairest option is on top.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
